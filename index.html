Questions, answered.

What is assisted stretching?

A practitioner stretches you on a table with guided, hands-on techniques. You reach positions that are hard to reach alone.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. The practitioner adapts every stretch to your body.

When do you open?

In 2026, in Zürich. Waitlist members learn the date and location first.

What does it cost?

We publish founding membership prices before we open in 2026. Waitlist members get them first.

Is it like a massage or physio?

No. Assisted stretching works on your range of motion. It is neither physiotherapy nor massage. We make no medical claims.
