Questions, answered.

What is assisted stretching?

A practitioner stretches you on a treatment table using guided, hands-on techniques — deeper and safer than stretching alone.

Do I need to be flexible?

No. Sessions meet your body where it is; most clients start with limited mobility.

When do you open?

Soon, in Zürich. Waitlist members get the date and location first.

What does it cost?

Founding rates will be announced at launch. Waitlist members get them first.

Is it like a massage or physio?

It's active, targeted mobility work — complementary to both, but focused on range of motion.
